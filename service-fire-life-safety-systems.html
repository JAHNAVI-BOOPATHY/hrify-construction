01 / OVERVIEWFIRE & LIFE SAFETY

Built on knowledge.
Designed for performance.

Fire and life safety planning considers how a building can help prevent, detect and respond to fire emergencies. Systems and escape provisions need to be coordinated with the building layout, occupancy and applicable authority requirements.

Fire and life safety provisions must be project-specific. Final design, equipment selection and approvals should be confirmed by qualified professionals and the relevant authorities.
