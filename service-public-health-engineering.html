Public Health Engineering

We plan water supply, plumbing, drainage and wastewater systems that support healthy, efficient and environmentally responsible building environments.

DISCUSS YOUR PROJECT ↗
01 / OVERVIEWPUBLIC HEALTH

Built on knowledge.
Designed for performance.

Public health engineering is essential to the safe and reliable operation of a building. It connects the systems that bring clean water into a development with the infrastructure that collects, treats and manages wastewater and stormwater.

Our goal is to coordinate essential water and sanitation infrastructure with the wider building design so that systems work efficiently together.
