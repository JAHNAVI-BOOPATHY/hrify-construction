Electrical Engineering

From power systems to lighting design, we plan electrical infrastructure intended to support reliable, safe and energy-conscious building operations.

01 / OVERVIEWELECTRICAL

Built on knowledge.
Designed for performance.

Electrical engineering provides the power and control backbone of a modern building. A coordinated design considers how electricity is received, distributed and used, while aligning lighting and essential services with the needs of the occupants.

Our electrical design approach aims to make building systems dependable, coordinated and appropriate to the operational needs of each project.
